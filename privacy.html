Effective Date: August 1, 2026 This Privacy Policy applies to the Grid-Pro Camera application (the "App") developed by the developer. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our application. 1. Information Collection and Use Grid-Pro Camera does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data. The App is designed to function entirely locally on your device. We do not use any tracking software, analytics frameworks, or third-party data collection tools. 2. Required Permissions To provide its core functionality, the App requests the following device permissions: Camera Permission: Required to capture photographs and record videos. The visual data processed by the camera sensor is strictly processed locally on your device in real-time. Microphone Permission: Required to capture audio while recording video. Audio is processed and saved alongside the video file locally on your device. Storage / Photos / Media Permission: Required to save your captured photographs and videos directly to your device's local storage (e.g., the DCIM/Camera folder). The App only writes media to your storage and does not scan, collect, or upload your existing files. 3. Data Processing and Storage All data processing, including but not limited to image signal processing, LUT (Look-Up Table) application, and video encoding, happens completely on-device. Your media files are saved directly to your local device storage. We do not have access to your photos, videos, or audio recordings. 4. Third-Party Services The App does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or ad networks that collect user data. There is no cloud synchronization or remote backup performed by the App itself. 5. Children's Privacy Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. 7.
